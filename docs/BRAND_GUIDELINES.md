# YUGON
## Official Brand Guidelines — Version 1.0

**Brand tagline:** Rooted in Tradition. Connected to Today.

**Brand positioning:** A modern spiritual ecosystem inspired by India's timeless traditions, built to make authentic spiritual knowledge, practices and communities accessible through technology.

---

# 1. Brand foundation

## Our vision
To create a connected spiritual world where timeless traditions, authentic knowledge and modern technology come together.

## Our mission
To make spiritual practices, verified religious knowledge, devotional experiences and access to trusted spiritual experts more accessible, convenient and meaningful.

## Our brand philosophy
Tradition deserves preservation. Technology should make it more accessible, never distort it.

## Our core values
- Authenticity: Respect verified sources and established traditions.
- Peace: Create a calm and welcoming experience.
- Trust: Be transparent about information, services and pricing.
- Inclusivity: Respect different regions, languages, traditions and sampradayas.
- Innovation: Use technology thoughtfully.
- Accessibility: Make spiritual experiences easier to discover and participate in.

# 2. Brand personality

YUGON should feel like a trusted Indian spiritual institution brought into the modern digital age.

| Attribute | Brand expression |
|---|---|
| Heritage | Indian cultural inspiration |
| Modern | Clean, intuitive technology |
| Authentic | Responsible religious knowledge |
| Peaceful | Calm and uncluttered |
| Premium | Refined, not extravagant |
| Welcoming | Accessible and inclusive |

YUGON must not appear overly commercial, excessively religious, superstitious or technologically cold.

# 3. Visual identity

## Primary colour palette

| Colour | Hex | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Temple Charcoal | #20251F | Primary dark backgrounds |
| Antique Gold | #B18A50 | Highlights and brand accents |
| Ivory | #F1EBDD | Primary light backgrounds |
| Terracotta | #9B684E | Warm secondary accents |
| Sage | #777B62 | Nature and calm |
| Muted Sand | #D9CBB2 | Secondary surfaces |

These are proposed brand colours and should be validated for accessibility before implementation.

## Colour usage
- Ivory should be the main light interface background.
- Charcoal should anchor the premium identity.
- Antique gold should be used selectively, not everywhere.
- Sage and terracotta should support secondary sections.
- Maintain readable contrast for every text and interactive element.

# 4. Typography

## Recommended typography system

- Headings: Cormorant Garamond
- Body and interface: Inter
- Devanagari: Noto Sans Devanagari
- Sanskrit and Hindi content: use appropriate script and verified transliteration conventions.

Headings should feel editorial and timeless. Interface text should remain clean and highly readable.

# 5. Logo system

## Primary logo
A custom YUGON wordmark with a distinctive emblem.

## Symbol concept
Combine three visual ideas:
1. Rising sun: A new age and the meaning associated with Yug.
2. Sacred geometry: Indian heritage and spiritual symbolism.
3. Contemporary geometry: Technology, connection and progress.

## Logo variations
- Primary horizontal wordmark.
- Stacked logo.
- Standalone emblem.
- Monochrome logo.
- Light-background and dark-background variants.
- App icon.

## Logo restrictions
- Do not distort proportions.
- Do not use unapproved gradients.
- Do not add unnecessary decorative religious symbols.
- Maintain clear space around the logo.
- Avoid placing the logo over visually busy photography.

The final logo artwork is a future design deliverable; this document defines its creative direction, not a completed trademark or logo asset.

# 6. Iconography and illustrations

Use simple, consistent line icons with balanced geometry.

Visual themes may include:
- Sun and light.
- Lotus and natural forms.
- Temple architecture.
- Sacred geometric patterns.
- Calendrical and astronomical symbols.
- Books, music and devotional instruments.

Avoid mixing unrelated icon styles or using decorative symbols where functional icons are needed.

# 7. Photography and visual storytelling

## Photography direction
- Natural daylight.
- Warm, authentic Indian environments.
- Real temples, architecture and cultural settings.
- Respectful depictions of rituals and devotional practices.
- Natural materials, textures and craftsmanship.
- Calm compositions with adequate negative space.

Avoid artificial-looking religious imagery, fabricated ritual scenes and misleading depictions of traditions.

# 8. Digital experience

## Website
The website should feel editorial, elegant, welcoming and intuitive.

## Mobile applications
The mobile experience should prioritise accessibility, simplicity and frequent devotional use.

## Interface principles
- Consistent navigation.
- Clear typography.
- Calm layouts.
- Accessible colour contrast.
- Thoughtful animations.
- Fast loading.
- Responsive layouts.
- Multilingual support.

The website and mobile apps should share the same design system.

# 9. Brand architecture

YUGON is the master brand.

Proposed product families:

- YUGON Puja — Puja guides and AI Pandit.
- YUGON Astro — Astrology and Kundli.
- YUGON Bhakti — Devotional music.
- YUGON Panchang — Panchang and Muhurat.
- YUGON Experts — Verified Pandits and astrologers.
- YUGON Community — Spiritual communities and gatherings.

All product families should maintain the primary YUGON identity.

# 10. Brand voice

YUGON communicates with warmth, clarity, respect and humility.

Preferred language:
- Simple and accessible.
- Respectful of cultural differences.
- Calm rather than sensational.
- Helpful rather than authoritative.
- Transparent about uncertainty.

Avoid:
- Guaranteed spiritual or astrological outcomes.
- Fear-based messaging.
- Unsupported religious claims.
- Claims that one regional practice represents all traditions.

# 11. Religious knowledge integrity

YUGON must distinguish verified religious knowledge from general AI-generated explanations.

- Ritual procedures, mantras, shlokas and samagri lists require approved sources.
- Tradition and regional differences must be preserved.
- AI must not invent missing ritual instructions.
- Uncertain answers should be identified as uncertain.
- Human expert escalation should be available where appropriate.
- Copyright and commercial permissions must be checked independently of source authority.

# 12. Brand application

The identity must remain consistent across:
- Website and mobile applications.
- Social media.
- Marketing campaigns.
- Expert profiles.
- Devotional music experiences.
- Community events.
- Email communications.
- Packaging and future physical products.

# Implementation note — colour contrast

Checked 13 November 2026 against WCAG 2.2, before the palette was applied to web and mobile.

| Pair | Ratio | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Charcoal on Ivory | 13.13:1 | Body text, links, focus outlines |
| Gold on Ivory | 2.67:1 | Decorative rules only. Not text, not focus. |
| Terracotta on Ivory | 3.94:1 | Large text or non-text accents |
| Sage on Ivory | 3.69:1 | Large text on Ivory, or non-text accents |
| Sand on Ivory | 1.35:1 | Secondary surfaces |
| Charcoal on Sand | 9.76:1 | Text on Sand cards |
| Ivory on Charcoal | 13.13:1 | Text on dark bands |
| Sand on Charcoal | 9.76:1 | Secondary text on dark bands |
| Gold on Charcoal | 4.92:1 | Gold wordmark on the charcoal footer |

Secondary interface text on Ivory uses `#4B4F40` (Sage mixed 51% toward Charcoal), which reaches 7.09:1. Shared tokens live in `packages/types/src/brand.ts`. The interim mark is a Cormorant Garamond wordmark. The emblem, app icon and full component library are still future deliverables.
