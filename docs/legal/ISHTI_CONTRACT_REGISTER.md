## 1. Executive Protocol

The **ISHTI Master Contract Register** governs all binding agreements, commercial contracts, employment arrangements, platform terms, and vendor covenants necessary to protect the platform from operational, intellectual property, financial, and regulatory liabilities.

> [!CRITICAL]
> **No commercial transactions, pandit consultations, or third-party data processing may occur without an executed agreement from this register.**

## 3. Contract Execution Workflow & Governance

1. **Dual Founder Sign-Off:** All contracts exceeding ₹5,00,000 in commitment or involving user personal data must be approved by at least two directors.
2. **Mandatory Standard Clauses:** Every commercial contract executed by ISHTI must incorporate:
   - Data Protection Covenants aligned with DPDP Act, 2023.
   - CERT-In 24-hour incident notification obligation.
   - Clear limitation of liability.
   - Intellectual property reservation protecting the ISHTI trademark and proprietary software.
3. **Repository Preservation:** Executed agreements (scanned signed PDFs and digital certificates) must be archived in an encrypted, access-controlled corporate legal drive with restricted access.
