## 1. Doctrinal Principle: The Two-Tier Scripture Rights Doctrine

Under the **Copyright Act, 1957** (Act No. 14 of 1957):
- **Tier 1 (Ancient Sanskrit Root Text):** The canonical Sanskrit texts of the Vedas, Upanishads, Bhagavad Gita, Puranas, and classical stotras were composed millennia ago and are firmly in the **universal public domain**. No individual, trust, or corporation can claim copyright ownership over the sacred Sanskrit shlokas.
- **Tier 2 (Modern Derivative Works):** However, every **modern translation, critical edition, analytical commentary, introductory essay, and sound recording** is an independent original literary or musical work protected by copyright under Section 13 and Section 14 of the Copyright Act for the lifetime of the author plus **60 years** (Section 22).

> [!CRITICAL]
> **RAG INGESTION SAFETY BARRIER:**
> It is strictly forbidden to scrape, vectorize, or ingest modern published commentaries or translations (e.g., Gita Press, Motilal Banarsidass, Ramakrishna Math, Chinmaya Mission, ISKCON) into the platform AI vector database without an **executed commercial license or written copyright assignment**.
> 
> Doing so constitutes prima facie commercial copyright infringement under Section 51, exposing the platform and its directors to statutory civil damages and criminal penalties under Section 63 of the Copyright Act.

## 3. Scriptural Rights Clearance Protocols

1. **Vetting Modern Commentaries:** Before any scripture translation or commentary is integrated into the production database or AI embeddings, the Head of Scriptural Curation and IP Counsel must inspect:
   - Date of author's death (must exceed 60 years for public domain status under Indian law).
   - Country of origin (under Berne Convention, rule of the shorter term applies).
   - Publisher claims over modern typographical arrangements.
2. **Performer Rights in Chanting (Section 38):** Reciting pandits possess statutory Performer's Rights under Section 38 of the Copyright Act. All studio recordings of Vedic chanting must be accompanied by an executed **Performer's Consent and Assignment Agreement** waiving moral and commercial performance claims in exchange for agreed honorarium.
3. **Canonical Fidelity Invariant:** In accordance with platform governance, verified public domain texts must never be truncated, altered, or bowdlerized to suit modern sensibilities. Sanskrit verses must preserve exact IAST diacritical marks and Devanagari ligatures.
