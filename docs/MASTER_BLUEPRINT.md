# Master Blueprint
## Purpose
Make authentic spiritual knowledge, religious practices, Panchang, devotional music and qualified human guidance accessible through one trusted platform.
## Six pillars
Puja and practices; Panchang and Muhurat; Astrology; Human experts; Devotional music; Communities.
## Principles
- Full feature parity is the target for web and Android/iOS.
- Religious accuracy and source rights are non-negotiable.
- AI is an interaction layer, not an authority that invents religious content.
- Regional and sampradaya differences must be preserved.
